Volunteerism in Canadian urban forestry: Results from the 2025 Canadian Urban Forest Census

Alexander Martin & Tenley Conway

Ontario Arborist, October/November 2026, pp. 34–36

An estimated 105,000 volunteers give roughly 379,000 hours a year to municipal urban forestry in Canada, mostly planting trees in parks and naturalized areas. Individual residents and local environmental groups are the most common partners, and municipalities with bigger budgets and more staff engage volunteers more.
