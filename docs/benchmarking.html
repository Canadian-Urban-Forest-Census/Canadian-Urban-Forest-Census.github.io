This page is under construction and shows mock data. Every figure below is a placeholder generated for testing the layout. Nothing here is a survey response, a modelled estimate, or a real value for any municipality.

Search any of the 343 urban municipalities in Canada to see a set of modelled estimates of its urban forestry program. Models were developed based on survey responses for staffing, budget, street tree work, and volunteer engagement.

    Under construction

    The comparison itself is still being built. Once it is ready, selecting a municipality will show how it sits against others of similar size, density and canopy cover across the measures collected in the 2025 census.

    In the meantime, the Methods page explains how the census was run, and the results published so far are on the Tree Inventories and Volunteers pages.

    The Canadian Urban Forest Census was supported through the Jack Kimmel Grant, awarded by the Canadian TREE Fund to Dr. Tenley Conway. Survey methods were approved by the University of Toronto Research Ethics Board (Protocol #48443).
